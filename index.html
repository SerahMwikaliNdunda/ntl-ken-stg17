    Where is measurable activity concentrated?

    National Sum of Lights grew +11.9% per year between 2015 and 2024. The light-to-population elasticity across ADM2 units is 0.50 (95% CI 0.17–0.82, R²=0.03): light is spread more evenly than population, suggesting broad-based service coverage.

    Who lives without detectable light?

    25,572,190 people — 46.7% of the inhabited population — live in cells that are inhabited but emit no detectable night-time light, against 31,909,101 in 2015. The largest concentrations are listed in the table; they are the natural starting point for a targeted off-grid or grid-extension programme. Depending on the noise floor (0.15–0.5 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹), this figure ranges from 24,218,348 to 29,673,054: that is the indicator's real uncertainty.

    How did the lit footprint change?

    The lit footprint moved from 11,519 to 42,731 km² (+271.0%), with 31,643 km² newly lit and 431 km² losing detectable light. The sprawl ratio of 2.68 indicates a pattern of outward spread rather than densification.

    Is light concentrating or spreading?

    Population-weighted inequality in light per capita across ADM2 units narrowed from 0.686 to 0.506 (Gini). 65% of total inequality sits between ADM1 regions and 35% within them. Initially dimmer units grew faster, indicating convergence.

    Which districts changed significantly?

    Median ADM2 growth in Sum of Lights is +23.4% per year. Growth is measured between the median of the first and last three years. 2 unit(s) exceed the robust +2.5σ threshold and 0 fall below −2.5σ; these are candidates for investigation, not confirmed findings — each should be checked against the gas-flare list, the product version and any known local event. 29 unit(s) with a very low initial base are shown but never flagged. Neighbouring districts grow alike (Moran's I = 0.32): flagged changes often cluster and should be read as regional rather than local phenomena.

    Limitations — read before citing

    • Thresholds: noise floor 0.25, urban core 10.0 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹; every 'unlit' figure depends on the first of these.
    • The brightest 1 % of pixels hold 66.2% of the national Sum of Lights.
    • Gas-flare candidates detected: 0. Verify against petroleum infrastructure before interpreting.
    • Night-time lights do not measure electricity access, GDP or population — every such reading is an inference.
    • Activity that emits no light is invisible here: subsistence agriculture, daytime informal trade, indoor activity.
    • No DMSP-OLS year is compared with a VIIRS year; the two are different physical quantities.
